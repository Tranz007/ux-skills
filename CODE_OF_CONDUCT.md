# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive.

UX Skills is intended for an open community of designers, researchers, engineers, product people, students, and AI practitioners. Critique ideas and implementations without attacking people. Assume good intent, make room for different levels of experience, and keep discussion focused on improving the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and abusive behavior are not welcome. Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that make the community unsafe or unproductive.

For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub.
